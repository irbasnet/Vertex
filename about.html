About Vertex

A family business, built one floor at a time.

How it started

What started at home became Vertex Architecturals.

Vertex Architecturals started small - doing quality epoxy and surface work for family and friends, taking the time to prep and finish every job properly without charging like it was a high-end commercial project.

Word travelled fast. People wanted a floor that looked considered without a commercial price tag, and there weren't many operators doing both well. That gap - between corner-cutting and overpricing - is exactly where Vertex Architecturals was built.

How we work

Preparation first, finish second.

A good surface is decided well before the final coat goes down. We start with an honest assessment of the substrate, moisture and movement in the space, and the conditions the finish will need to withstand.

From there, the finish - plain, metallic, textured or an exterior pavement surface - is chosen to suit the space, not the other way around.
