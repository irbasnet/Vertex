Pathway Paving

Overview

Considered surfaces, outside.

A front entrance sets the tone before anyone reaches the door. We finish pathways, pedestrian walkways and exterior pavement with the same material care we bring indoors - surfaces built to handle weather and foot traffic without losing their character.

From a straightforward entrance path to a fuller courtyard or driveway apron, the approach is matched to the site and the way it's used.

Suitable applications

  • Front entrances
  • Garden and pedestrian pathways
  • Courtyards
  • Driveway aprons

Finish options

  • Exposed aggregate
  • Stencilled and pattern paving
  • Natural paver laying patterns
