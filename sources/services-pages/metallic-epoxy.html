Epoxy Flooring

Metallic Epoxy

Overview

Flowing pattern, depth and movement.

Metallic epoxy builds on a plain epoxy base with pigments that move and layer as they're applied, producing flowing, marbled patterns with genuine depth. No two floors read quite the same way - the movement of the material is part of the finish.

It suits spaces where the floor is meant to be noticed: a feature living area, an entertaining space, or a showroom floor that needs to make an impression.

Suitable applications

  • Feature living and entertaining areas
  • Home bars and media rooms
  • Showrooms and reception spaces
  • Statement commercial floors

Finish options

  • Custom colour blends
  • Varying metallic density and movement
  • High-gloss protective topcoat
