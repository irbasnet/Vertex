Epoxy Flooring

Overview

Seamless, durable, considered.

Epoxy flooring is the foundation of our indoor finishes work - a seamless, joint-free surface engineered to handle real daily use while reading as a single, considered plane.

We offer three approaches depending on the space: a clean plain finish, an expressive metallic pour, or a tactile textured system. Each starts from the same disciplined preparation and finishes to the same high standard.

Suitable applications

  • Garages and carports
  • Living, utility and laundry areas
  • Showrooms and retail floors
  • Warehouses and commercial facilities

Finish options

  • Plain - matte, satin or gloss sheen
  • Metallic - flowing, marbled pattern and depth
  • Textured - broadcast aggregate or flake grip
