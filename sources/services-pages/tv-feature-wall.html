TV Feature Wall

Overview

A feature wall, finished like a floor.

The media wall is one of the most-viewed surfaces in the home, and it's rarely given the same material attention as the floor beneath it. A resin-finished feature wall brings depth, sheen and a seamless joint-free surface to the space around your screen.

We're currently finalising finish options and full project gallery for this service - get in touch to discuss an early project.

Suitable applications

  • Living room media and TV walls
  • Home theatre feature walls
  • Bar and entertaining feature panels

Finish options

  • Plain and metallic resin finishes
  • Custom colour and tone matching
  • Integrated cable and mount detailing
